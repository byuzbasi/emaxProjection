# emaxProjection 0.1.0

Initial public source and documentation publication; not a CRAN release.

- RcppArmadillo kernels for the closed shape, integral, derivatives, fixed-shape
  slices, analytic envelope, and adaptive projection.
- Contrast and full mean-region calibration with unchanged study assumptions.
- Feasible witnesses, endpoint brackets, empty-set accounting, unresolved
  feasibility, and explicit ordinary-double-precision diagnostics.
- Deterministic examples and aggregate clinical reproduction materials.
- Documentation website, independent R reference functions and aggregate-data reproduction example.
